Fraction Express

Drive the train. Deliver every fraction parcel to the right stop on the number line.

How to play

  1. Pick a parcel.
  2. Move the train to the stop where that fraction belongs.
  3. Press Drop parcel.
  4. When all the parcels are on the line, press Check.

Using a keyboard? Tab moves between buttons. Enter picks. The arrow keys drive the train.

Choose a level

For teachers

Pupils place fractions on a number line, then compare them. Stars are awarded only for parcels placed correctly first time. Parcels placed wrongly return in a second chance round.

  • Level 1, Year 3: compare and order unit fractions, and fractions with the same denominators.
  • Level 2, Year 4: recognise equivalent fractions on a number line.
  • Level 3, Year 5: compare and order fractions whose denominators are all multiples of the same number.
  • Level 4, Year 6: compare and order fractions, including fractions greater than 1.

The game needs no internet connection and does not ask for, store or send any information about pupils. Nothing is remembered once the page is closed.
